The One Day Bot Program

One program day a week at each school. This is what we do on and after each day.

Chapter 4.2.2.1 · Week 2 · Email Campaigns

Email Campaigns Every Weekend

Segmented by region and sent on Saturday and Sunday, from week 2 onwards.

2,000emails a month
Sat + Sunsend days
By Stateone segment per state
In the Email Tool
  • Opened and not opened, per state
  • Clicked, and which link was clicked
  • Bounced: wrong or full inboxes
  • Unsubscribed and spam reports
  • Replies from principals
In Google Analytics
  • UTM tags on every link: source, medium, campaign
  • Users and sessions from each campaign
  • Landing pages they read, and for how long
  • Key events: demo booking, call or WhatsApp click
  • City and region of the visitors
Every Monday
  • Compare click rate by state
  • Read the clicked links: what interests schools
  • A/B test one thing: subject, send time or opening line
  • Clean bounced and unsubscribed leads
  • Plan next weekend's segments

Judge by clicks, not opens. Some mail apps mark emails as opened automatically, so the open rate runs high.

Chapter 4.2.2.2 · Week 2 · Social Posts

One Video and One Image Post Every Week

Each post goes on every channel: Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn.

What We Post
  • 1 video: robots in action, live school demos
  • 1 image or content post: robot photos, science facts, tips
  • Behind the build: kits, trainers, the AI tutor
  • Principal and teacher quotes
  • Captions in English and the state's language
What We Measure
  • Reach and impressions
  • Video: views, average watch time, % watched
  • Image post: likes, comments, shares, saves
  • Profile visits and link clicks (UTM-tagged)
  • Followers by region, DMs and enquiries
Senior Marketer Notes
  • Hook in the first 3 seconds
  • Post when parents are online: evenings and weekends
  • Tag the city and state in every post
  • Count enquiries per post, not likes
  • Test a small paid boost of the best post in one state

Chapter 4.2.2.3 · Week 2 · Calls

50 Calls Every Day

Every call aims for one thing: an appointment with the principal.

Two Call Lists
  • New schools from the lead database
  • Follow-ups: callbacks, after an email click, after a visit
  • Ask for a 20-minute appointment with the principal
Log Every Call
  • Who answered: principal, coordinator or other
  • Outcome: no answer, call back, interested, appointment booked, not interested
  • Appointment date and time, or the next call date
  • One line of notes
Conversions We Watch
  • Connect rate: calls answered
  • Decision-maker reached
  • Appointments booked per day
  • Follow-ups done on time
